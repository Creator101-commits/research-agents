How values are calculated: Flows, prices and factors come from the Python run: manure sent to the digester, digestate and compost N/P/K, biogas, electricity valued at displaced farm demand, heat value, avoided grid CO₂e, the L4 feed saving (returned dry matter) at the workbook feed prices, water price, grid emission factor and carbon-credit price (0 unless a scenario sets one). ROI = (Lifetime Benefit − Total CapEx) / Total CapEx × 100. NPV discounts annual net benefit at the selected rate. Payback = Total CapEx ÷ Annual Net Benefit.
Scenario estimates (not model values, not farm quotes): capital costs per cow or per kWe ($700/cow digester, $3,500/kWe CHP + 15% install, $180/cow separator, $350/cow solar, $280/cow whey unit, $220/cow water recycling, $120/cow sensors, $35/cow beef program), digestate N $1.20/kg and P $2.50/kg, compost and water N/P/K $0.80/kg, manure disposal $3/tonne, biogas displacement 1.5 kgCO₂e/m³, CHP demand-charge saving 25% and energy-security premium 5%, whey disposal $40/tonne and functional premium $0.08/L on half the whey, irrigation value 2.5× the water price, treatment $0.50/m³, vet saving $50/cow and culling benefit 1.5% of milk revenue.

Cow Efficiency Ranking

Every cow in the herd ranked by Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) — kg of feed per kg of milk produced. Lower FCR = more efficient cow. Efficiency % is scored within the herd's actual FCR spread (1–99%). Tiers are assigned from that score: Elite ≥ 80% · Good 60–79% · Average 35–59% · Poor < 35%.
